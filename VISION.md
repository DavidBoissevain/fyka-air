# Vision

**fyka-air** is an open source, Dutch-sovereign app for monitoring air quality in the Netherlands.

The Netherlands already has rich, open air quality data: official RIVM measuring stations plus thousands of citizen sensors, collected through [Samen Meten](https://samenmeten.rivm.nl/dataportaal/). The tools for exploring that data are functional but dated. fyka-air puts the same data in a modern, beautiful interface that anyone can use and check.

## Principles

- **Open source.** The code, the data processing and the methods are public, the same way the underlying data is.
- **Dutch sovereign.** The data comes from Dutch public sources, and the app runs on infrastructure under Dutch or EU control, without depending on non-EU platforms where that can be avoided.
- **Modern and beautiful.** It's built with shadcn/ui. Clear maps, charts and summaries make air quality easy to understand at a glance, on any device.
- **Honest about the data.** It separates official measurements from citizen sensors and raw values from calibrated ones, and it says how fresh each reading is.
