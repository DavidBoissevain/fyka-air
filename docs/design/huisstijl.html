How Fyka Air looks, reads and shows air quality

Fyka Air uses the same base as the other Fyka apps: steel neutrals, navy dark mode and the three-wave mark. On top of that it has its own sky accent, the LKI colour scale for data, and rules that show where each value comes from and how old it is.

Principles

Four promises, and what they mean on screen

Honest

Every value shows its source, whether it is calibrated, and its age. A number never appears without a unit and a time.

Calm

Colour is for air quality. The interface itself stays in steel neutrals so the LKI colours carry the meaning. The sky accent never appears on the map.

Dutch

Dutch copy, Dutch number format and Dutch public sources: RIVM, PDOK and Luchtmeetnet. Write 6,8 µg/m³, not 6.8 ug/m3.

Open

Methods are public, so the interface links to them. Every index and colour can be explained in one sentence on the page.

Logo

The Fyka mark with a sky tile for Air

Fyka Air follows the app mark recipe from fyka-website: a tile with 25% rounding, a one-hue gradient and a white line icon at 60% of the tile. The Air glyph is Stroom: two air currents and a particle, drawn on the 24-unit grid. In code it is AirMark in components/air-mark.tsx, and app/icon.svg is the favicon.

Rules

  • Write the name as Fyka Air, with a capital F and A.
  • Keep the app mark at 24 px or larger. Below that, use the Fyka mark.
  • Don't recolour the three waves; they belong to the whole family.
  • Don't put the mark on the map or on an LKI colour.
Colour

Steel for the interface, LKI for the air

Each swatch shows its light and dark value side by side. Click a swatch to copy its light-mode hex.

Fyka base, from fyka-website

Steel scale

The shared Fyka neutral scale. Use it for borders, secondary text and charts that don't show air quality.

Sky accent

Fyka Air's own colour, for buttons, selected tabs, links and focus. It stays off the map, so nobody mixes it up with the blue of LKI 1 to 3.

Luchtkwaliteitsindex (LKI)

The RIVM index runs from 1 to 11 in five categories. These colours are only for air quality values: map dots, the index tile and chart thresholds. Use the category name next to the colour, never the colour alone.

Typography

Figtree for everything, Geist Mono for identifiers

Figtree is friendly without being casual, and its numbers stay steady in tables. Geist Mono is only for station codes, datastream names and anything people may copy.

Data honesty

Source, calibration and age, every time

These rules come from the decision log in TECHNICAL.md. They are part of the style, not an extra.

Gemeten 23 min geleden

Under 1 hour old. Normal display.

Gemeten 2 uur geleden

Between 1 and 3 hours. Amber dot, and the age stays visible next to the value.

Niet meer op de kaart

Over 3 hours. The sensor leaves the map, but its page and history stay available.

Official or citizen

Official stations are large dots with a halo. Citizen sensors are small dots without one. The badge in the panel names the source in words.

Calibrated first, raw on request

Show RIVM's calibrated value by default. A switch shows the raw value next to it, never instead of it. Click a small dot on the map to try it.

Map

A quiet basemap in both themes

The app uses MapLibre GL with the PDOK BRT-Achtergrondkaart. PDOK has no dark style, so Fyka Air ships its own dark variant with these colours. The map on this page draws CBS province borders from PDOK as a stand-in for the full basemap.

Station locations: 41 real measuring stations from Luchtmeetnet (RIVM, GGD Amsterdam, DCMR). Province borders: CBS gebiedsindelingen 2023 via PDOK. Citizen sensors, readings and index values on this page are example data. The LKI category ranges follow RIVM's index. The hex values are Fyka's own choices in RIVM's colour order; check them against the official RIVM legend before launch.
